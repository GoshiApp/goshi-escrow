# Security policy

Goshi moves funds, so security reports matter a lot.

**Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.** Write to **support@goshi.app** with "SECURITY" in the subject, and include what you found, how to reproduce it and the impact.

- We acknowledge a report as soon as we can and keep you informed until it is fixed.
- Please give us reasonable time to fix before you disclose, and do not access or move other people's funds while testing.
- The program has not been audited by an external firm. Treat it accordingly.

Scope: the Solana program, the game server, the website and the signing flow. Out of scope: denial of service by volume, social engineering and third party services.
