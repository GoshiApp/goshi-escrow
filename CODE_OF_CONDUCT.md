# Code of Conduct

We want Goshi to be a welcoming place for everybody who takes part, whatever their experience, background or country.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient, give and accept feedback with kindness, assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the community.

**Not accepted:** harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, spam, and promotion of scams or of financial promises.

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads and block people who break these rules. To report a problem write to support@goshi.app. Reports are handled in private.
